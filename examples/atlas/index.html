Design-Space Atlas

One runnable case for every design the Screengrid grammar can express — every intent, every comparison scope, every aggregation, glyph, mark, layout, scale and palette. Each card shows the spec that produced it, the validator's verdict on that spec, and the legend the compiler derived. Nothing here is hand-drawn: every map is validateSpec → compileSpec → render.

The gridded-glyphmap technique and its design space are due to Aidan Slingsby (IEEE VIS 2023, doi:10.1109/VIS54172.2023.00009). Screengrid's contribution is the executable formalization: a declarative grammar, machine-checkable cartographic validation, and semantic cells carrying provenance, reliability and comparability. This atlas is the evidence for that claim — and tests/unit/DesignSpaceCoverage.test.js fails if any case stops being demonstrated.

All data is synthetic and seeded: the same map renders on every run. Cells are screen cells at the current view, never fixed geographic districts — pan any map and watch them re-form.
