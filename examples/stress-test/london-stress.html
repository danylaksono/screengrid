How the library holds up rendering large point datasets. Synthetic London points from 5k to 500k; pan/zoom to re-project and re-aggregate every frame and watch the FPS. Compare square vs hex tessellation and colour-fill vs per-cell glyph.

FPS is a rolling average of the render loop while the map is moving. Every pan/zoom re-projects and re-aggregates all points from scratch, so this is a worst-case for screen-space binning. The glyph reads each cell's raw records (cell.cellData) directly — the most efficient route — to measure raw render throughput, not semantic-summary cost.
