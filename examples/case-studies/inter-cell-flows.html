Inter-cell flow lines

A different glyph idea: instead of a summary inside each cell, each cell draws arcs to the other end of its trips — origin cells reach toward the cells their trips arrive in. Both ends snap to cell centroids, so the whole flow network re-forms as you pan and zoom (coarser cells → thicker, fewer arcs). It is deliberately a bit of a jumble; the structure is the point.

Screen-space glyphs draw on one shared canvas, so a cell's callback can draw beyond its own bounds — no library change needed. In the spirit of Slingsby's gridded glyphmaps and Wickham's glyph-maps. …
