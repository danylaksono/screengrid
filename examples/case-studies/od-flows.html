Origin–destination flows as gridded glyphs

Each screen cell aggregates the trip origins that fall inside it and draws a flow rose: a petal per compass direction, its length the number of trips heading that way, its colour the mean trip length. Pan and zoom — the cells re-bin to the view, so the roses are analytical bins, never fixed zones.

Technique: gridded glyphmaps, Aidan Slingsby (IEEE VIS 2023). The global/local reference scaling follows Wickham, Hofmann, Wickham & Cook, Glyph-maps for visually exploring temporal patterns in climate data (2012). …
