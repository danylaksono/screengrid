ScreenGrid Examples

A curated tour of density grids, hex aggregation, glyph maps, temporal glyphs, geometry anchors, and domain-specific visual analytics.

Declarative Grammar

Grammar 01 · Density Pipeline The full profile → intent → validate → compile → render pipeline on ~40k synthetic London points, with a live validation report. Grammar 03 · Composition Glyphs Pie glyphs whose slices come from each cell's lazy semantic measures, with the category-count guardrail and a sparse-cell cue.

Case Studies
