# Screengrid Research Ideas

This folder collects paper-planning notes for positioning Screengrid as a multivariate cartographic design pattern and reproducible grammar.

## Working Thesis

Screengrid should not be positioned as "a new glyph map" in isolation. The safer and stronger claim is:

> Screengrid is a reproducible, data-agnostic grammar and browser-based authoring workflow for constructing, validating, and sharing screen-space gridded glyph maps for multivariate point data.

The intellectual lineage should explicitly credit Aidan Slingsby's gridded glyphmaps. The novelty should be argued around generalisation, grammar, reproducibility, semantic cell summaries, cartographic validation, uploadable data workflows, and reusable design patterns.

## Central Risk

The work is close to gridded glyphmaps. A paper will be vulnerable if it claims novelty at the level of "grid cells plus glyphs". It becomes much more publication-worthy if it demonstrates a reproducible grammar, a semantic cell model, validation rules, and multiple data-agnostic case studies that show how users can design maps from their own uploaded data.
